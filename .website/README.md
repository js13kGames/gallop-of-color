🌈 **The world has lost its color — time to bring it back!** Race from crystal to crystal, build your power, and paint the world with every gallop. 🦄

# Controls

*Best played with a controller!*

| | Controller | Keyboard / Mouse |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Left Stick | WASD |
| Camera | Right Stick | Mouse |
| Jump / Double Jump | Bottom Face Button<br>(XBox 🟢 A / PS5 🔵 X) | Space |
| Gallop | Right Trigger | Shift |

# Gameplay

- **Collect crystals** and color in the world to fill your power meter.
- When you aren't coloring in the world, your power goes down.
- Fill your power meter to gain power-ups indicated on the meter.
- **2x, 3x, and 4x** are score multipliers,  **◎+** increases the size of your coloring area.
- **When you collect the final crystal, the run ends**
